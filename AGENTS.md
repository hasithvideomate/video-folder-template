## User camera, captions and background rules
For generated footage and edits, never add zoom-ins, zoom-outs, digital punch-ins or automatic reframing. Keep the camera and framing locked. Never add on-screen captions, subtitles, speech text, titles or text overlays. Keep the reference background stationary: no drifting, moving, morphing or animated walls, posters, furniture, equipment or screens; no background parallax or flicker. Preserve natural presenter articulation and phrase-matched gestures while the room remains still. Put these requirements in every future video-generation prompt and editing plan. Independent review must check actual temporal evidence for camera/scale changes, caption overlays and background movement; any observed violation needs correction, and unavailable evidence remains uncertain. Existing physical reference posters are scenery, not added captions, and must remain fixed.
