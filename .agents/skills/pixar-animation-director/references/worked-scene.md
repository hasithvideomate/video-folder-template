# Worked example: The Second Cup

Formatting demonstration using the illustrative source below; assets are not generated. Settings remain model-dependent. Never substitute this story for a user's supplied script.

Illustrative source: A caretaker robot sets two cups for breakfast and looks at an empty chair. It reaches toward the second cup, stops, and lowers its hand. A damp bird ruffles its feathers outside the window. The robot notices it and opens the window. The bird settles near the warm cup. The robot relaxes. No dialogue.

Premise: A caretaker robot lays out breakfast for an absent friend, notices a bird shivering outside, and shares the warmth it was saving.

Turn: ritual of absence → hesitant connection. Do not make grief mandatory in every project.

CH01: small pear-shaped copper robot, dark oval glass eyes, short arms with three broad fingers, cream scarf, worn matte metal. ENV01: compact kitchen, round central table, window screen right, morning light from that window. PROP01: two cream ceramic cups, one at robot's place and one by the empty chair; track them separately.

Total used duration: 25 seconds. Generate supported clip lengths and trim. Split bird landing or window opening further if needed.

Directing interpretation: stay with the robot's experience until its interrupted gesture has registered; withhold the bird insert until then. The locked close view lets a tiny hesitation carry the beat. The inferred feeling of absence is a performance interpretation, not permission to invent a death or backstory. The robot's fixed glass eyes and shoulder/hand poses carry attention without adding human eyebrows or tears. Keep the same pale window light throughout; do not brighten the room magically at the ending.
