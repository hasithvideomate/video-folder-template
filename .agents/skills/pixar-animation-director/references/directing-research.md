# Public research and application

Verified 2026-10-10. Use paraphrases and distinguish public evidence from production recommendations.

| Public evidence | Skill application |
| --- | --- |
| Scanlon describes his family experience as the starting point for Onward, with its world enabling the story. | Start with a specific emotional premise; design the world to serve it. |
| Pixar in a Box distinguishes wants and needs. | Build action around goals and growth around underlying needs. |
| Its storyboarding exercise visualizes staging, framing, and motion through simplified drawings. | Test clarity and performance in rough boards. |
| Its story-reel lesson combines boards with sound to experience sequences over time. | Test holds, cuts, and emotional transitions in an animatic. |
| Catmull's firsthand Braintrust excerpt describes candid collective feedback. | Diagnose problems openly while preserving the user's directing judgment. |
| Pixar in a Box's dynamic-shots lesson connects camera movement to the audience's experience of a moment. | Choose a camera move for a specific dramatic purpose; do not add motion by default. |
| Its color-script and master-lighting lessons plan light/color across the film and then establish lighting within scenes. | Record a color/light progression and a stable scene lighting plan before individual-shot variation. |
| Official Inside Out 2 production notes describe distinct movement choices for Anxiety and camera behavior associated with her influence. | Develop character-specific performance and camera choices; do not copy that character's behavior into unrelated scenes. |

Reference bibles, IDs, clean keyframes, continuity ledgers, pilot generations, and controlled retries are this skill's AI adaptations; do not attribute them to Pixar. General animation principles are animation craft, not exclusive Pixar practices.

The scene-decision fields, listening guidance, voice/timing records, source-fidelity audit, and ordered review passes in `directing-decisions.md` are a practical synthesis, not claims about Pixar's internal procedures. ## Primary sources

- [Pixar in a Box](https://www.pixar.com/pixar-in-a-box): official curriculum entry point.
- [Onward](https://www.pixar.com/onward): Scanlon's firsthand account of family inspiration.
- [Wants vs. needs](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/character/a/activity-2b): Pixar/Khan Academy character exercise.
- [Storyboarding](https://en.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/film-grammar/a/exercise-5-film): staging, framing, motion.
- [Storyreels](https://en.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/v/pitching4): sequencing drawings with sound.
- [Inside the Pixar Braintrust](https://www.fastcompany.com/3027135/inside-the-pixar-braintrust): Ed Catmull with Amy Wallace, excerpt from Creativity, Inc. Publicly accessible text establishes candor; do not invent details from inaccessible sections.
- [Dynamic shots](https://www.khanacademy.org/v/dynamicshots): camera movement and emotional engagement; publicly indexed lesson transcript.
- [Color scripts](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/art-of-lighting/introduction-to-virtual-lighting/v/colorscripts): planning light, color, and value across scenes; publicly indexed lesson transcript.
- [Master lighting](https://en.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/art-of-lighting/introduction-to-virtual-lighting/v/master-light): color keys and shared scene lighting; publicly indexed lesson transcript.
- [Inside Out 2 production notes](https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/inside_out_2_production_notes_updated_final_05-23-2024_f2901ceb.pdf): official Disney-hosted notes on performance and layout. These are film-specific examples, not universal prescriptions.

## Provider documentation

- [Runway image-to-video guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/48324313115155-Image-to-Video-Prompting-Guide): images establish visual facts; motion-oriented text directs changes. Source artifacts may intensify in video.
- [Google Cloud Veo 3.1 guide](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/ultimate-prompting-guide-for-veo-3-1): cinematography, subject, action, context, atmosphere; references, endpoint frames, and audio where supported.

Treat named versions and limits as historical documentation; verify current endpoint capabilities during production. Do not claim one universal prompt-length rule: Runway suggests starting simply and refining; Google demonstrates structured detailed control. Adapt to the chosen model/interface and observed output.
